Claude

A family of AI assistants built by Anthropic

What is Claude?

Claude is a large language model (LLM) developed by Anthropic, an AI safety and research company. Claude is designed to be helpful, honest, and harmless, and can assist with a wide range of tasks including writing, analysis, coding, summarization, research, and conversation.

Key Capabilities

  • Natural, context-aware conversation across long documents.
  • Writing and refactoring code in many programming languages.
  • Summarizing, analyzing, and extracting information from text.
  • Reasoning through complex, multi-step problems.
  • Working with images, documents, and structured data.

The Claude Model Family

Anthropic offers several Claude models tuned for different needs:

Opus

The most capable model, built for deep reasoning and complex tasks.

Sonnet

A balanced model offering strong performance with high efficiency.

Haiku

The fastest, most lightweight model for quick, responsive tasks.

Built with Safety in Mind

Anthropic focuses on AI safety research, using techniques such as Constitutional AI to help Claude behave according to a set of guiding principles. The goal is to make AI systems that are steerable, reliable, and aligned with human values.
